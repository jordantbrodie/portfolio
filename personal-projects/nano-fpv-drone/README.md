# Nano long-range FPV drone (planned build)

**Personal project with a friend, planned April–May 2026.** A fully 3D-printed FPV quadcopter that runs on a single 18650 Li-ion cell, based on the open "Improved Nano Long Range Drone" design on MakerWorld (by piodeer).

What I prepared:
- **Plan:** budget (€433 including goggles and radio), shopping list, assembly steps, wiring checks and a test sequence (indoor hover → vibration check → PID tuning → first outdoor flight). [Plan (PDF)](Nano_FPV_Drone_Plan.pdf)
- **Parts and mass breakdown:** every component with weight, size, price and material. The battery is about 47 % of the take-off weight, so its position sets the centre of gravity. [Breakdown (PDF)](Nano_FPV_Drone_Parts_Breakdown.pdf)
- **Split of work:** one person on 3D printing and mechanical assembly, one on electronics, wiring and configuration; both on testing and tuning.

Build photos and flight results will be added here.
